# Season-specific Team Match

Open Play → Pick game & scope → Team Match. Choose a league, then select a
**Roster season** and one or more teams. The default **Current teams** option
keeps using the legacy current-team links.

Seasonal questions use the chosen season's Campaign memberships (Activations),
plus roster observations when the optional roster catalog tables are installed.
Repeated observations of the same player/team pair count once. Players listed
with multiple teams in that season are excluded, including conflicting teams
outside the selected team filter. This avoids asking a single-answer question
with multiple valid answers. Coverage is limited to the recorded source data;
selecting a season does not imply a complete historical roster.

Today's active/inactive flag does not filter historical memberships. The season
and team answers are saved with each round. Resume, results, replay setup and
round-miss drills preserve that context. Cross-round missed-name drills reuse
saved questions when available, so a player transfer does not change the answer.
No current-team assignments or source roster records are rewritten.

Season selection currently applies to Team Match. Guess the Division continues
to use current classification; switching to it clears the historical season.
