# Browse entity pages

Browse groups the catalog into sports data, places, quests and ratings, and time. Public entity pages keep the visitor's current Play, Feed, Moderate, or Admin side, subject to the account's existing access. A public catalog link does not grant Admin access.

Sports and place overviews show related records in named sections with full counts. Where a section has a **View all** link, the link opens that entity's scoped directory. Player previews show up to 12 records. Sport team previews and country and state city previews also show up to 12 records. Country stadium previews and league competition previews also show up to 12 records. Other related sections display all their records.

City and state pages label their birth-place player associations **Players born here**. Team profiles provide their current player lists. Venue pages retain address and capacity. Missing metadata displays **Not recorded**, and empty related sections name the missing record type.

Year pages link to recorded seasons. Season pages retain status, dates, teams, campaigns, comments, and seed metadata. The season card and its champion have separate links. Season, campaign, and league overviews distinguish competitions from individual games. Competition pages show their format, titles, and player appearances when recorded, and link to their games.

Organization pages retain current teams, dated affiliation history, and sourced staff appointments. Affiliation, membership, contract, and activation pages retain their dates and related entities. Stored JSON details wrap within their panels.

The catalog uses the shared heading, panel, action, and color tokens in light and dark themes. Existing sorting, filters, directory pagination, rating controls, attribution, and moderation visibility rules remain in place. Achievement and spectrum actions retain their existing permissions. Player and team corrections and account management remain in Admin. This pass adds no management permissions or editing endpoints.
